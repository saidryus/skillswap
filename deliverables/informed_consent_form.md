# Informed Consent Form
## Acadia: A Web-Based Peer-to-Peer Academic Tutoring Platform
**College of Computer Studies | University of Cebu Pardo-Talisay Campus**

---

### Purpose of the Study

You are invited to participate in a research study evaluating **Acadia**, a web-based peer tutoring platform developed by students of the College of Computer Studies (CCS) of the University of Cebu Pardo-Talisay Campus. The purpose of this study is to assess the platform's usability, usefulness, and effectiveness in supporting peer-assisted learning.

This evaluation is part of an academic research study conducted in partial fulfillment of the requirements for a degree program at the University of Cebu Pardo-Talisay Campus.

---

### What You Will Be Asked to Do

If you agree to participate, you will:

1. Be assigned a user role (Student-Tutee, Student-Tutor, or Administrator) and provided with login credentials for a test account.
2. Complete a set of assigned tasks using the Acadia platform, which will take approximately **20–30 minutes**.
3. Complete a short written survey (via Google Forms) after the testing session, which will take approximately **5–8 minutes**.
4. Allow researchers to observe and take notes on your interactions with the system during the session.

---

### Voluntary Participation

Your participation in this study is entirely **voluntary**. You may withdraw at any point during the session without consequence. Choosing not to participate or withdrawing from the study will have no effect on your academic standing or relationship with the institution.

You may also skip any survey question you are not comfortable answering.

---

### Risks and Benefits

There are **no known physical, psychological, or academic risks** associated with this study. The tasks involve only using a web-based application on a computer. No sensitive personal information will be collected beyond what is necessary for the study.

Your participation will contribute to improving a platform designed to support student learning within your institution and across similar Philippine academic programs.

---

### Confidentiality and Data Privacy

In compliance with **Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012)**:

- You will be assigned a pseudonym (e.g., Participant 1, Participant 2) in all research documentation and reports. Your real name will never appear in any published output.
- All data collected — including observation notes, survey responses, and interaction records — will be stored securely and accessed only by the research team and the research adviser.
- Test accounts used during the session contain no real student academic records. All data used in the evaluation is either fabricated or anonymized.
- Your responses and data will be used solely for this academic research study and will not be shared with any third party.
- You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data collected about you after the completion of the study.
