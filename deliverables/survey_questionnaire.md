# Survey Questionnaire — Acadia Peer Tutoring Platform
**Usability Evaluation Study**
University of Cebu Pardo-Talisay Campus | College of Computer Studies

---

## Instructions to Participants

This survey is part of a research study evaluating the usability and effectiveness of the **Acadia** peer tutoring platform. Your responses will help the researchers understand your experience with the system and identify areas for improvement.

- There are no right or wrong answers. Please respond based on your actual experience during the testing session.
- All responses are **strictly confidential**. You will be identified only by a pseudonym (e.g., Participant 1) in all reports.
- You may **skip any question** you are not comfortable answering.
- This survey is distributed via **Google Forms** through Facebook Messenger after the testing session.
- Estimated completion time: **10–15 minutes**
